HELD-OUT VALIDATION

MTI was evaluated against conventional and data-driven comparators.

Standardization and model fitting were restricted to training folds across 100 repeated stratified 5-fold partitions. The combined MTI + LVEF model had the highest primary discrimination point estimates, but superiority over LVEF was not established.
